How we worked it out

  • Carbon per fitting = its weight × a carbon factor for its material. The factors come from the ICE database (v4), e.g. particleboard 0.664 kg CO₂e per kg; prices are the low end of Malaysian retail prices.
  • Carbon (and material) only counts where at least {{ '{:.0f}'.format(offer * 100) }}% of owners remove that fitting. Below that we can't say the buyer would have ripped it out, so their choice saves money but counts no carbon.
  • SDP's saving = installation it no longer pays for, minus the credit it gives the buyer. Upgrade income = SDP's margin on each upgrade.
  • Reused fittings count their carbon, minus the delivery trip ({{ transport }} kg CO₂e per km, both ways).
  • Advice: removed by {{ '{:.0f}'.format(stop * 100) }}% or more of owners → stop installing; {{ '{:.0f}'.format(offer * 100) }}–{{ '{:.0f}'.format(stop * 100) }}% → offer as an opt-out (or as an upgrade, for items like tiles that can't be left out); fewer → keep.
