How the projection works

  • Homes that would rip a fitting out = homes × that fitting's rip-out rate for this home type.
  • Of those, the uptake share choose before handover: the fitting is never made, so its weight and carbon are avoided.
  • SDP gains = installation it skips minus credits paid, plus its margin on upgrades, minus the running cost.
  • Rehomed fittings count their carbon, minus a {{ trip }} km delivery trip each way ({{ transport }} kg CO₂e per km).
  • "Pays for itself after" = running cost ÷ SDP's gain per home.
