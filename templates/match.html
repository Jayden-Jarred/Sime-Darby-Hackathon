How it works

Simple rules, so it can run inside SDP's existing owner app or portal.

  1. Give what you removedOnly fittings you reported removing, with their condition and a note. Tiles and toilets aren't listed: hacked-out tiles break, and toilets aren't reused for hygiene reasons.
  2. Same home type onlySame design, same measurements, so it fits with no measuring or rework.
  3. Nearest neighbour firstA new offer goes to the nearest neighbour looking for one, or you can answer a post yourself. Each trip's carbon ({{ trip_factor }} kg CO₂e per km, both ways) is taken off the saving.
  4. Collect and say thanksIt only counts once it's picked up: then its material is reused and its carbon, minus the trip, is saved.
